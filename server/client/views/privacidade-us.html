Privacy Policy (United States)

This policy describes how we handle information for US-based users and highlights health-data practices that may fall under HIPAA and state privacy laws.

Last updated: April 2026

HIPAA awareness: If PulseFlow creates, receives, maintains, or transmits protected health information (PHI) in connection with covered entities or business associates, we implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards consistent with applicable requirements. Specific roles (covered entity, business associate, or neither) depend on your use case and contractual arrangements.

Information we collect

We may collect account and profile data (e.g., name, professional identifiers such as NPI or state license, contact information), usage data, and health-related content that you or authorized users submit to provide the service.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and secure the platform
  • To authenticate users and enforce access controls
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations
  • To communicate service-related notices

Safeguards

We use encryption in transit (e.g., TLS), access controls, logging, and vendor diligence proportionate to the sensitivity of data processed. Workforce members with access to PHI receive appropriate training where applicable.

Rights and choices

Depending on your state of residence, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or opt-out of certain processing. HIPAA grants patients specific rights regarding their PHI when held by covered entities; direct those requests to the appropriate provider or entity when applicable.

International transfers

If data is processed across borders, we apply safeguards consistent with applicable law.
